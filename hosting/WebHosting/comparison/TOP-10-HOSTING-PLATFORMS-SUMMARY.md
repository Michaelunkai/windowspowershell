## ⚡ QUICK DECISION GUIDE

### Choose **Cloudflare Pages** if:
- ✅ Performance is critical
- ✅ Need unlimited bandwidth
- ✅ Want free analytics
- ✅ Global audience

### Choose **Vercel** if:
- ✅ Building with Next.js
- ✅ Need ISR capability
- ✅ Want best-in-class DX
- ✅ Preview comments important

### Choose **Netlify** if:
- ✅ Need form handling
- ✅ Want extensive plugins
- ✅ Need identity service
- ✅ A/B testing required

### Choose **AWS Amplify** if:
- ✅ Already using AWS
- ✅ Need full backend
- ✅ Enterprise compliance
- ✅ Complex authentication

### Choose **GitHub Pages** if:
- ✅ Documentation site
- ✅ Zero budget
- ✅ Simple static site
- ✅ Open source project
